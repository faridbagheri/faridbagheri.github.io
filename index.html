About

Research with an engineering mindset.

My work sits at the intersection of natural language processing, knowledge representation, recommender systems and LLM engineering. I focus on systems where model outputs must be not only accurate, but also explainable, auditable and useful in real decision-making workflows.

Research

Grounded, interpretable and auditable language-model systems.

I investigate how structured knowledge and semantic representations can make LLM-based retrieval and recommendation systems more reliable, transparent and easier to evaluate.

01

KG–LLM Integration

Hybrid pipelines combining LLM-generated semantic signals with graph-based representations for stronger matching, grounding and reasoning traces.

02

Reviewer Recommendation

Content-based reviewer assignment using semantic enrichment, similarity scoring and ranking metrics including MRR, MAP and Precision@K.

03

LLM Evaluation

Evaluation for relevance, factuality, coherence, hallucination risk and instruction compliance, including multi-model and judge-based selection.

  • Evaluation with MRR, MAP and Precision@K
