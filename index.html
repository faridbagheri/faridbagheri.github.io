About

Research with an engineering mindset.

My work sits at the intersection of natural language processing, knowledge representation, recommender systems, and LLM engineering. I focus on systems where model outputs need to be not only accurate, but also explainable, auditable, and useful in real decision workflows.

Research

Grounded and auditable language-model systems.

I investigate how structured knowledge and semantic representations can make LLM-based retrieval and recommendation systems more reliable and interpretable.

01

KG–LLM Integration

Hybrid pipelines that combine LLM-generated semantic signals with graph-based representations for stronger matching and clearer reasoning traces.

02

Reviewer Recommendation

Content-based reviewer assignment using titles, abstracts, summaries, keyword extraction, similarity scoring, and ranking metrics such as MRR, MAP, and Precision@K.

03

LLM Evaluation

Evaluation of LLM outputs for relevance, factuality, coherence, hallucination risk, and instruction compliance, including judge-based model selection.
