From text to a KG–LLM matching pipeline

Engineering · LLMs · Knowledge Graphs

A hybrid reviewer-matching pipeline can combine language-model signals with structured graph representations instead of relying on only one representation method.

1. Start with document content

Candidate papers and reviewer publications provide titles, abstracts, and summaries. These fields become the semantic input to the system.

2. Extract semantic signals

An LLM can extract domain-specific keywords. Additional components can enrich the representation with concepts and entities.

3. Build structured representations

Open information extraction can create relations that are represented as graphs. Graph-embedding methods such as Node2Vec can then transform structural information into vectors.

4. Compare paper and reviewer profiles

Similarity functions such as cosine similarity and Jaccard similarity provide transparent matching signals. Multiple signals can also be combined into a hybrid score.

5. Evaluate the ranked output

The final step is ranking reviewers and evaluating results with metrics such as MRR, MAP, and Precision@K. This closes the loop between representation choices and measurable recommendation quality.
